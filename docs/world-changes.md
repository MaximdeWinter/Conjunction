# Hide areas

A hide area is an outline you draw in the world. While the player is inside it, the game hides the grass, the ground
or the water there, as you set it. Use it for a cellar, a cave or an arena of your own. It works whenever the location
it belongs to is shown, for everyone who has the quest or place mod installed.

## Drawing one

**Project** > **Places** > **Hide area**. Click the corners of the outline in the world (at least three), **Enter**
finishes. The area is a row of the location. Select it to see its switches:

- **Foliage** (on for a new area): grass, bushes and trees are hidden while the player is inside.
- **Terrain**: the ground is hidden while the player is inside. Something of yours has to stand there, or the player
  looks into the void.
- **Water**: the water is hidden while the player is inside.
- **height**: how far up from its lowest corner the area reaches.
- **Outline** shows the corners again in the world. Drag a corner to move it, click a line for a new corner,
  right-click a corner to remove it.

A hide area belongs to its location: the game hides these things while the location is shown. You see it in the game
after **Build & Play**.
