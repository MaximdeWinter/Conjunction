# Hide areas

A hide area is an outline in the world. While the player is inside it, the game hides the grass, the ground or the
water there. For a cellar, a cave or an arena of your own.

## Drawing one

**Project** > **Places** > **Hide area**. Click the corners in the world (at least three), **Enter** finishes. Select
the area for its switches:

- **Foliage** (on for a new area): grass, bushes and trees
- **Terrain**: the ground. Something of yours has to stand there, or the player looks into the void
- **Water**
- **height**: how far up from its lowest corner the area reaches
- **Outline** shows the corners in the world: drag a corner to move it, click a line for a new corner, right-click a
  corner to remove it

The game hides these things while the area's location is shown. You see it after **Build & Play**.
