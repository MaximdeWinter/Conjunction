A profile is optional. With one, everything you make with Conjunction carries the history of who made it. Without one, nothing is signed (Settings > Profile > No profile). This is how it works.

Your profile

  • A profile is a name and a key. The key was made on this PC together with the profile. It is the file below.
  • The name is whatever you choose: your real name, a nickname or anything else.

What gets signed

  • Every step of a project (made, edited, built, exported) goes into its history with the name, the tools and the time. The key signs each step.
  • Quests stay open to editing, people can change other people's quests and mods made with Conjunction. Each change becomes a new step in the history that says what and who changed it.
  • The history travels inside the built quest and the .w3q. Anyone can read it. What nobody can do is rewrite it: a step changed, taken out or added afterwards will show in the history as an error.
  • Conjunction also leaves a mark of the key in the quest itself, too small to see. It stays when someone removes the history. Check origin (Settings > Profile) will find it.

The key file

  • It is the only proof that something is yours. Keep it and back it up. On a new PC, copy it into Documents\Conjunction\identity.
  • Never post it or send it to anyone, not even to show who you are. Whoever has the file can sign as you, and then nothing signed with it proves anything any more.
  • A lost key is gone for good. A new key cannot sign as the old one.

Staying anonymous

  • Only the name is shown, never the key. Nothing in the history points to you unless the name does.
  • Conjunction sends nothing anywhere. Signing works without the internet. The one exception is Public timestamp on export (Settings > Profile, off unless turned on): it sends a fingerprint of the history to opentimestamps.org, which proves when the quest existed. The fingerprint holds neither the name nor the quest.
  • A second profile (Settings > Profile > New profile) has a key of its own. Nothing links two profiles, so work under different names cannot be traced to the same person.
